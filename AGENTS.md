# Project architecture decisions

- The homepage has no intro loading screen: it opens straight into the hero, and the hero's GSAP entrance runs on mount. Don't re-add a pre-load curtain or the assemble intro videos — they streamed ~8 MB before first paint and tanked mobile performance.
- Homepage visual effects use GSAP (ScrollTrigger, ScrollToPlugin) for scroll-driven choreography and Motion for component presence and hover, plus CSS. hls.js streams the homepage background video and is imported on demand in the browser only. Keep off-screen pausing and reduced-motion fallbacks, and don't add further animation runtimes.
- Deep research "signals" step also gathers press news (Google News RSS), social profiles by web search, and one ledger-billed Claude web-search company profile (brands, key people, customers, reviews, ads, events); every AI item must cite a URL the search really returned, and social networks are never fetched directly — keeps budget caps, blocks invented facts, avoids scraping platforms that forbid it.
- The deep research "Atlas" tab only regroups facts the run already verified (src/lib/deep/atlas.ts, pure and client-safe); new atlas data must arrive as cited facts from a pipeline step, never be added in the UI — keeps one source of truth and the anti-hallucination rule.
- The hero paints its still poster first; the live ASCII vortex engine starts only after window load + idle (later on phones), and below-the-fold homepage sections (films, consultation, pricing) are lazy-loaded — keeps mobile first paint fast.
