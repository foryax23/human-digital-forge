# Vortex Hub homepage — cinematic premium refresh

## Direction
Rebuild the homepage around the selected **Cinematic dark premium** direction:

- **Palette:** Midnight Electric — `#070713`, `#17152E`, `#665CFF`, `#5FE3D0`, `#F5F7FF`
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk for headings, DM Sans for body and interface text
- **Structure:** full-width cinematic story bands with fewer boxes and clearer visual hierarchy
- **Feel:** precise, premium and high-trust; motion supports the story instead of obscuring content

## What will change

### 1. Foundation and navigation
- Replace the current purple-heavy styling with the locked Midnight Electric token system.
- Load Space Grotesk and DM Sans, then establish consistent type scale, spacing, borders and focus states.
- Refine the sticky header into a quieter, compact navigation with clearer active states and a stronger primary action.
- Preserve the working EN/RO switch, login/dashboard destination and all existing navigation routes.

### 2. Hero rebuilt for immediate clarity
- Make **Vortex Hub** and its actual offer the first unmistakable signals.
- Use a concise headline, supporting copy and two decisive actions without animated blur on essential text.
- Recompose the existing product mockup as a wide, inspectable stage rather than a dim decorative object.
- Add restrained depth, information-path animation and pointer response; simplify or disable these effects on mobile and reduced-motion devices.
- Replace the dense five-item strip with a cleaner proof rail that remains readable at every size.

### 3. Stronger selling narrative
- Reorder and tighten the page into a clear progression:
  1. Offer and proof
  2. Core services
  3. Who it helps
  4. How delivery works
  5. AI automation capability
  6. Selected work
  7. Consultation decision bridge
  8. Four plans
  9. Trust and final action
- Remove repeated claims and reduce excessive vertical length while keeping every existing business offer.
- Give each section one dominant visual idea instead of repeating the same dark card treatment.

### 4. Section redesigns
- **Services:** a cinematic core-offer composition with one dominant service and two supporting capabilities; brighter images and clearer outcomes.
- **Audience:** two wide editorial stories rather than paired card grids.
- **Process:** a connected delivery timeline with animated progress signals and better mobile sequencing.
- **AI automation:** an information-flow demonstration that visually explains trigger → processing → outcome.
- **Portfolio:** larger, brighter case-study imagery with outcome-led captions and a clear portfolio action.
- **Consultation:** remove placeholder language and present the three session types as a confident decision bridge.
- **Pricing:** preserve Free, Starter, Growth and Pro prices/features while improving comparison, emphasis and mobile scanning.
- **Trust:** combine duplicated proof content, keep the dynamic icons, and remove the empty testimonial placeholder.
- **Final action/footer:** use the Vortex media as a controlled brand moment, then simplify the footer and legal navigation.

### 5. Motion, backgrounds and transitions
- Retain the existing performant canvas system, but use one effect per major story beat instead of stacking effects.
- Introduce deliberate section transitions that shift from near-black to violet depth and mint signal accents.
- Use staged reveals, subtle parallax, animated data paths and tactile hover depth.
- Pause off-screen effects, cap display density, and preserve static reduced-motion fallbacks.

### 6. Cookie experience and responsive finish
- Turn the cookie collector into a compact bottom panel on desktop and a non-blocking mobile sheet so it no longer hides the hero.
- Preserve Necessary, Analytics and Marketing choices plus the existing settings reopening behavior.
- Verify typography, actions, mockup framing, pricing and navigation at desktop and mobile sizes.

## Technical details
- Homepage presentation files only, plus shared header/footer/cookie styling where visible on the homepage.
- Keep the existing TanStack routes, bilingual content system, authentication links, checkout behavior and legal pages unchanged.
- Use semantic design tokens rather than hardcoded component colors.
- Add no new animation framework; continue with Motion, CSS and the existing optimized canvas utilities.
- Validate compilation, browser console, mobile/desktop layout, reduced motion, language switching and the pricing action states.

## Completion criteria
- The first viewport clearly communicates the brand, offer, proof and next action.
- Images and text remain legible without relying on hover or animation.
- The page is materially shorter, more varied and easier to scan.
- All four plans, both languages, navigation, cookie choices and calls to action still work.
- No overlapping content, blocked mobile hero, console errors or broken routes.
